With Dwabit out sick (!?) Deli and Scout must put up with Captain Martinez on the rush mission to tow the crashed 
spaceship used by the scientists of the research outpost on the planet Faolaoran.

Scout and Deli learn that, for one thing, Captain Martinez does not know how to pilot a ship. Secondly, 
Dr. Fromanian, a scientist at the Faolaoran outpost, has pulled strings with his uncle to get a space tug out the 
Clump system to get the outpost's ship back to Port Rails. Scout later learns that Dr. Fromanian had a personal 
connection with Captain Martinez as well.

Captain Martinez piloted the ship through the strait into the Clump system, and retired to his bunk to take his 
sleep shift. (Actually, Deli piloted the ship through the strait, while making Captain Martinez believe he was doing it.)

In Clump, Deli calls the outpost to see what is going on. Deli speaks with Dr. Lance Umbeko, who is alone in the 
outpost. He informs her that Dr. Maria Alexandrich and graduate student Solar Unixi took the outpost's hovercar 
across the surface of the planet to where the Blue Eagle had crashed.

Dr. Umbeko also reported that Dr. Fromarian had communicated to the outpost and Dr. Alexandrich that he had arranged 
pickup and that he was going to wait for them. There was no need to come and "save him."  Dr. Umbeko had expected Dr.
Alexandrich to turn around and return to the outpost. Umbeko had been studying some of the data he had collected, 
and lost track of time. At the time Deli called, he was surprised to discover that Dr. Alexandrich and Solar weren't back yet. 

Dr. Umbeko called the hovercar on the radio, and Dr. Alexandrich stated she was continuing to the crash site to keep Dr. Fromanian company while he waited. She expected a ship coming out from Gaia to take at least 3 or 4 days.
