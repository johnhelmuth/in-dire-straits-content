With Dwabit out sick (!?) Deli and Scout must put up with Captain Martinez on the rush mission to tow the crashed 
spaceship used by the scientists of the research outpost on the planet Faolaoran.

Scout and Deli learn that, for one thing, Captain Martinez does not know how to pilot a ship. Secondly, 
Dr. Fromarian, a scientist at the Faolaoran outpost, has pulled strings with his uncle to get a space tug out the 
Clump system to get the outpost's ship back to Port Rails. Scout later learns that Dr. Fromarian had a personal 
connection with Captain Martinez as well.

Captain Martinez piloted the ship through the strait into the Clump system, and retired to his bunk to take his 
sleep shift. (Actually, Deli piloted the ship through the strait, while making Captain Martinez believe he was doing it.)

In Clump, Deli calls the outpost to see what is going on. Deli speaks with Dr. Lance Umbeko, who is alone in the 
outpost. He informs her that Dr. Maria Alexandrich and graduate student Solar Unixi took the outpost's hovercar 
across the surface of the planet to where the Blue Eagle had crashed.

Dr. Umbeko also reported that Dr. Fromarian had communicated to the outpost and Dr. Alexandrich that he had arranged 
pickup and that he was going to wait for them. There was no need to come and "save him."  Dr. Umbeko had expected Dr.
Alexandrich to turn around and return to the outpost. Umbeko had been studying some of the data he had collected, 
and lost track of time. At the time Deli called, he was surprised to discover that Dr. Alexandrich and Solar weren't 
back yet. 

Dr. Umbeko called the hovercar on the radio, and Dr. Alexandrich stated she was continuing to the crash site to keep Dr.
Fromarian company while he waited. She expected a ship coming out from Gaia to take at least 3 or 4 days.

Scout and Deli take their sleep shift, leaving Captain Martinez in charge of the ship, which was running on automatics
with no expectation of issues.

Dr. Fromarian contacts the tugboat and demands that Captain Martinez hurries up, that there will be consequences if the
doctor does not get off planet soon.  Captain Martinez pushes acceleration higher, without realizing that this will
force the team to have to stop to refuel on the way back. They should arrive at Faolaoran 2 hours earlier.

Deli discovers the call from Dr. Fromarian, and she and Scout discuss what it means. They decide to sandbag the process
of hooking up to the *Blue Eagle* to give Dr. Alexandrich time to arrive at the crash site. If everything works out, the
hovercar should arrive while they are preparing to leave the planet, the _Blue Eagle_ in tow.

On arrival, they saw that the _Blue Eagle_ had landed too near a cliff face, that had collapsed on the rear of the ship.
They saw there had been some equipment set up near the ship that had been buried under the rocks and dirt from the cliff.

Talking with Dr. Fromarian, they discovered that his graduate student, Grady Segrave, had been in his bunk while they
had been doing some core sample extraction near the cliff, when the cliff collapsed on the engine compartment of the 
ship, killing Grady. Dr. Fromarian had been working in the lab near the front of the ship when the cliff collapsed. He
had been analyzing the samples as they were extracted.

As Scout connected the _Tugboat_ to the _Blue Eagle_, Deli took recordings of the area. She examined the parts of 
the sampling equipment that had not been buried. It appeared to be an automated core sample extractor that moved 
cylindrical core samples into a sample box. The sample box had been removed from the equipment before the _Tugboat_ 
had arrived on the scene.

She went into the _Blue Eagle_ to also record the damage inside the ship, per standard procedure. She interrupted a
conversation between Captain Martinez and Dr. Fromarian in the common room, who stopped talking when she cycled through 
the airlock. After questioning why she was there, they told her to hurry up. She noticed what was probably a sample box 
from the core sample extractor tucked under a counter in the common room of the _Blue Eagle_. 

Recording the rear of the ship, the engine had been completely crushed by the rockfall. Grady's bunk was just in front
of the engineering compartment, and she had to force the bunk door open. Grady had been crushed, apparently while 
sleeping; she needed a body bag to extract his remains after taking recordings.

When she left the rear part of the ship with the video equipment and Grady's remains, the Captain and the Doctor had 
already cycled out the airlock. She noticed the sample box was no longer tucked under the counter.

Scout had taken as long as possible to hook the _Blue Eagle_ up to the _Tugboat_. He did not see the Captain and the
Doctor move between the two ships.

As everyone loaded into the _Tugboat_ to take off, the hovercar with Dr. Alexandrich and Solar Unixi arrived.

Dr. Fromarian did not want to talk to them, Captain Martinez went out to talk to them. He convinced them that everything
was all right. ???
