Dwabit and Scout arrived to the annual Harvest Party at The Loreto, sponsored by the Port Rails chapter of the Vat Clone
Veterans of Foreign Wars Association and the Port Rails Port Workers Union. The Loreto was crowded with vat clones of
all ages, port workers, and their guests. Remy was busy at the bar, smiling and chatting with the crowd, and Trinity, as
usual, sat in their booth in the back corner, a large smile on their face.

Scout spotted Dr. Ogubre at the far end of the bar; hoping to avoid her, she moved to a group of vat clones chatting on
the other side of the bar.

Dwabit, upbeat because knew he was no longer a lone Tardigrada, was dressed in his new traditional
Tardigrada clothing, a button-down shirt that hung to his ankles, patterned in brightly colored geometric patterns. He
elbowed his way through the crowd and hopped up on an empty stool at the bar to order a drink for Trinity, as he often
did. Next to him was Lina Bershsvin, a private security officer he knew, and next to her was another short human he had
never met, short black hair pulled back in a bun, well-dressed. The young human is surprised at Dwabit, her eyes
widening in shock. She whispers into Lina's ear, andDwabit only catches one word, "mumble mumble? WHAT mumble?". He
ordered a drink for himself and Trinity's usual, a Martini Twist. He took the Martini Twist over to Trinity and chatted
for a few moments.

Scout overheard another vat clone in the group, Cheri Hilside, talking about a job she was heading out to in the
morning. During the war, Cheri had worked for the NED Navy Intelligence as an operative. Pressing her further, Scout
learned that she was going to be working for NKC on Cyron, and that she couldn't really talk about it. Scout convinced
her that Scout would be a good addition to the team. Cheri gave the details to meet her the next morning.

Dwabit is in a such a good mood and nostalgic that he's compelled to talk to the other union folks about how the union
wasn't always the given institution it is now, that they had to fight for it, and reminisces about a particular strike
and withstanding the attack of the ol' Pink Jackets. Mara overhears this, and shouts "It is you! Dwabit!" before quickly
exiting the bar.
