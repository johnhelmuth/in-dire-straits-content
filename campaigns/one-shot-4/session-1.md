The [Space Merchant Trading Federation](/tatterpedia/factions#space-merchant-trading-federation-smtf)
has discovered an artifact on the airless second moon of the gas giant
Glaorkg V, in [cluster CSCID a88](/map/local-neighbor-cluster-coreward). You are
part of the large team that the SMTF assembled to research the artifact. The
team is made up of over a hundred scientists, engineers, security personnel,
pilots, and other support staff.

You are part of the team selected to explore the artifact's potential.

When you arrived on Glaorkg V-b, your shuttle landed near a cliff that was the
edge of a large crater. You walked in your vacsuit from the shuttle toward the
cliff and entered what appeared to be a lava tube, about 10 meters in diameter.
20 meters within the lava tube a bulkhead with a large airlock had been
installed. Beyond that, the lava tube continued in a “shirt-sleeves”
environment. Living quarters, offices, and labs had been bored out in side
passages from the main lava tube. 300 meters down the lava tube another bulkhead
had been installed with an armoured pressure hatch leading to the cave
containing the artifact.

The artifact is a large bronze colored cube, 15 meters on a side, engraved with
glyphs covering most of the surface, sitting in the center of circular chamber,
60 meters across.

Dr. Ridley aan Caast-Foundling made the breakthrough in deciphering the glyphs,
remembering a report recorded thousands of years ago about the discovery of a
lost colony, Maaraat, inhabited entirely by Ohlzor and So’enala. The glyphs
matched the writing and language the Ohlzor on that planet used. That colony was
eventually integrated into known space, although the language used by the Ohlzor
on the colony at that time has been completely replaced by Galacticom in the
intervening centuries.

Eventually, the artifact was discovered to be a device that could create a
direct connection to a similar device on another planet somewhere in the galaxy,
enabling matter to be sent between the two planets without having to travel
across the star system to a strait point and shoot the strait. The scientists
named it a Straitgate, as it used straits in slipstream space in a way similar
to the slipstream drive. The trip would be one way, as the portal closed 35
seconds after the last matter that entered the Straitgate was transferred.

Testing of the artifact showed that probes could be sent across to the other
planet. The Straitgate could be re-opened in the other direction after the power
system on Glaorkg V-b was recharged. The data recorded by the probes on the
other side of the Straitgate indicated a chamber similar to the one on Glaorkg
V-b, a 60 meter wide circular chamber, with the exception that directly across
from the Straitgate was a rockfall that blocked the obvious exit from the
chamber. The probes showed that the atmosphere, temperature, and gravity in the
chamber was sufficient for most sapients to survive without vacsuits, if
necessary.

Multiple test animals were also sent across multiple times, and all retrieved
without harm. Now is the time for sapients to cross and learn what opportunities
await on the new planet beyond, and you were offered a position on the team to
do so.

You accepted for your own reasons.

## Mission Report

The team stepped through the Straitgate and shot the strait in their vacuum
suits. Officer Fria Sun described it as a tingling while walking through a space
filled with shattering mirrors without being cut. The arrived in the dark
Straitgate chamber as expected, facing the rock fall blocking the entrance to
the chamber.

They did not arrive at their destination without loss, though. Shortly after
stepping through the Straitgate, _Captain Burinn Skreek_ and _Tech Specialist
Carn Arty_ fell to the ground, experiencing seizures and expiring before Officer
Fria Sun could apply medical help.

Dr. aan Caast-Foundling assisted Officer Fria Sun in determining the cause of
the deaths of their teammates.

Dr. Emelmahay and Engineer Kaolars examined what appeared to be a control panel
of some sort half buried in the rock fall.

Officer Avery Johnson checked around the perimeter of the chamber, his suit
lights revealing finished stone walls. As the chamber wall opposite the rock
fall came into sight, Officer Johnson noticed a stocky bipedal figure moving
through a crack. Announcing to the rest of the crew that he had contact and was
in pursuit, he followed the figure through the crack, revealed to be a door 
fashioned to look like part of the wall. 

He chased him down into a cavern filled with stalagmites and stalactites. As he
saw the robed figure pass a large stalagmite, he saw a tall figure attack it...
