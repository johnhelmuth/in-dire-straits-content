## Lasana

Lasana is a Port Rails neighborhood on level 37, sector 17, surrounding the central lifts. It consists of retail businesses and apartments serving lower to middle class workers.

## The Loreto

Aspects: ***Where everyone knows your name*** ; ***“Honey, I think this is a … vat clone bar!”***

The Loreto is located in the Lasana neighborhood on level 37 sector 17, near the center lifts. It is accessed from the main passageway by a small staircase leading down to the front door. It is decorated with warm faux oak panels and understated lighting. There is an oval bar in the center of the space and tables scattered around it. There is a hallway in the back leading to private rooms.

Seated at the bar facing the door are Harley Paulsen and Garnet Alder, sisters or close cousins, hunched over their beers. There is a bouncer keeping an eye on the room, and chatting with one of the waitresses. There is a bartender cleaning glasses and serving beers and mixed drinks.

As you enter, a person with average height and build, dark skin with blue eyes and a shaved head, moves towards you with a smile on their face and a twinkle in their eye. “Welcome, my dears, to the Loreto\! I am Trinity Ocano and I hope you enjoy your time here.” They reach out with both hands to grasp yours in greeting.

## Hotel Baltimore

The misnamed Hotel Baltimore is a bar in the Lasana neighborhood on level 37 of Port Rails.

Aspects: ***Old Earth Elegance in Faux Mahogany and Steel*** ; ***New Policy: Check Your Weapons at the Door***

Before entering the Hotel Baltimore, the doorman discretely scans all customers for weapons and directs those carrying to the weapon check room to the right of the anteroom. The weapon check girl will take their weapons, lock them up in individual lockers. and return a weapon check ticket. If cybernetically enhanced individuals have built-in weaponry, they will be asked to wear an inhibitor to disable the deployment and use of their weapons in the facility.

As you enter the main room of the Hotel Baltimore you can see a long bar along the left wall, tables scattered throughout the middle of the room, booths against the back wall, and a small stage on the right wall where DJ Liam is playing some music from old Earth. There are 3 obvious bouncers in black suits standing along the walls or pacing through the room. Waitstaff, wearing white button-down shirts, black vests and pants, talk softly with their customers.

### Faces in this place

* Bartender
* Owner
* Professor DJ Liam McRaven
* Waitstaff
* Bouncers
