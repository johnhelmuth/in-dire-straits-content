---
title: The Ancient Wolves
---

Street gang of teens and young adults from lower class families with some older members.

Their territory is on level 37, extending from the center lifts of sector 16 to the edge of sector 17\.

They wear black jackets with blue denim pants, boots with steel “claws” on the toes. The jackets have a howling wolf head / moon logo on the back, and on the left breast.

They run protection rackets, deal drugs, petty theft, and extort tolls from the unlucky who run across them in their territory.

They typically are not enhanced with cyberwear.

They prefer nightsticks with a snarling wolf head on the head, although they have been known to carry knives and firearms as well.
