title: Isolated Cluster Factions
description: A list of factions in the Isolated Cluster.
---


* Factions
  * [Port Rails Port Authority](/tatterpedia/factions/isolated-cluster/port-rails-port-authority)
  * Port Rails Security
    * Port Rails Security (or just station Security) are on Floor 25, Sector 17.
  * [The Ancient Wolves](/tatterpedia/factions/isolated-cluster/ancient-wolves)
  * Fronk’s Salvage Service
    * Smallish salvage service. Will be frequent bidder on auctions for
      impounded vessels. Source of parts and black market stuff.
  * Patriot Security
    * Private security group
    * Often at odds with Station Security
  * Blackadders
    * Teen “gang” on Port Rails.
    * Mostly kids of middle and upper class parents who like getting into
      trouble.
    * Their “colors” are kilts of
      the [Scottish Blackadder clan tartan](https://www.scotclans.com/blogs/clans-b/clan-blackadder-tartans),
      green and blue, with black and white accents.![](/images/torrin/blackadder_gang_colors_500x.jpg){align="center" size="xx-small"}
  * Vat Clones Veterans of Foreign Wars Association (VCVFWA) ![](/images/torrin/harley-paulsen-vat-clone.jpg){size="x-small" align="right" caption="Harley Paulsen, VCVFWA Port Rails Chapter President"}
    * Vat clones have gotten together and formed an association to support them
      as they age. The chapter for Port Rails meets at The Loreto in the
      Lasana neighborhood.
    * Harley Paulsen is the Port Rails chapter of the VCVFWA
  * Space Workers Union
    * Confederation of separate unions for specific specialties of space
      workers; they are all members of the SWU.
    * Some associated Unions
      * Insystem Shipworkers Union of Torrin
      * Insystem Shipworkers Union of Cyron
      * Portworkers Union
  * Steel Revolution
    * A lot of former Cyron soldiers from the war got together and formed the
      Steel Evolution, ostensibly to liberate Cyron from NED’s tyranny.
    * They use illegal warframes, that were supposed to be removed as part of
      the treaty that ended the war.
  * Nomia Kolpuh Corporation (NKC, or just Nomia)
    * Interstellar corporation based in Cyron that is trying to recover from the losses in marketshare they suffered from the war.
    * NKC has divisions focused on various markets: Technology, Agriculture, Pharmaceuticals, Transport, Astronautics, Medical, Energy, Security, others.
  * Committee for the Completion of the Ring (CCR)
    * Grassroots organization dedicated to expanding Port Rails to its original design as a full planetary ring.
